# Historical concept decision

- **Role:** historical rationale, not current roadmap.
- **Decision recorded:** the early selection of Peekaboo Pockets and the interaction models considered around it.

The project compared concepts by the new form of agency they offered, not by how easily they could be reskinned. A viable experiment needed immediate feedback, imprecise-input tolerance, bounded state, optional caregiver participation, offline operation, and no requirement for reading, sound, speed, or correctness.

## Interaction models considered

| Model | Intended agency | Historical disposition | Current relationship to the product |
|---|---|---|---|
| Stable pockets and doors | Open, close, revisit, remember | Selected as the third slice | Became Peekaboo Pockets and later expanded into stable search, clues, emergence, and reunion. |
| Stack and settle | Arrange large pieces spatially | Preserved | Became Stack & Settle. |
| Child-led rhythm | Create and revisit a short sequence | Initially deferred | Reframed without playback demands as Together Tones. |
| Broad veil reveal | Gradually uncover one stable scene | Preserved | Still uncommitted; belongs in `IDEAS.md`, not the active roadmap. |
| Free-drawn route | Author a path another object follows | Preserved with motor-access concern | Still uncommitted; sustained dragging may exclude younger children. |
| Soft shape homes | Relate a piece to size or shape | Revisit after observation | Still uncommitted; visible non-matches can become wrong answers. |
| Path-tile builder | Rotate pieces into a route | Deferred | Remains too abstract and precision-heavy for the current age hypothesis. |
| Counting or tracing task | Reproduce a quantity or line | Rejected for this age slice | Correctness, language, and precision conflict with the current product contract. |

## Why Peekaboo was selected

At the time, Bloom created visible objects and Color Splash transformed a connected region. Stable pockets added a third state model: reversible visibility while identity and location remained stable. The first implementation deliberately excluded shuffling, wrong-cup play, scoring, timers, narrated questions, and automatic new rounds.

The current game is deeper than that first decision: it now includes themed searches, one stable target, a lively clue, emerged friends, greetings, and deliberate replay. Current behavior belongs in `PRODUCT.md`; future Peekaboo work belongs in `GAME_ROADMAPS.md`.

## Evidence boundary

External child-development and technology guidance informed the original preference for active, child-controlled exploration and caregiver proximity. It did not validate Peekaboo or establish a learning outcome. See `PRODUCT.md` for current sources and `EVIDENCE.md` for product-specific evidence.
