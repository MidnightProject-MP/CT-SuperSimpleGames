# Idea incubator

This file contains only uncommitted directions that have not earned a place in the active portfolio or per-game roadmaps. Ideas here are hypotheses, not promises or current behavior.

When an idea becomes planned, move its working definition to `ROADMAP.md` or `GAME_ROADMAPS.md` and remove the competing description here. When it ships, current behavior belongs in `PRODUCT.md` and evidence belongs in `EVIDENCE.md`.

## Broad veil reveal

Touching or broadly wiping could uncover one stable picture in forgiving patches. Every contact would reveal something and previously revealed areas would remain visible.

The open question is whether gradual reveal creates meaningful anticipation or merely repeats Color Splash with a different visual skin. It should not proceed unless its agency feels distinct without requiring sustained dragging.

## Child-authored path

A child could place broad route marks that a friendly object follows. The interesting concepts are direction, anticipation, and revising a path—not tracing accuracy.

The open question is whether tap-authored segments can make the experience complete for children who cannot sustain a drag. A continuous-line requirement would conflict with the current motor-access principles.

## Soft shape homes

Large objects and flexible homes could explore relative size, nesting, and containment. Every placement would need a satisfying consequence, including imperfect or non-matching placement.

The open question is whether differences can remain exploratory rather than becoming a disguised right-answer sorter. This should not proceed if the core pleasure depends on correction.

## Promotion rule

Promote an idea only when it introduces a distinct form of agency or answers an unresolved product question better than extending an existing game. Asset novelty alone is insufficient.

Earlier Story Scenes, Peekaboo, and Color Splash proposals have been implemented or incorporated into `GAME_ROADMAPS.md`; they are intentionally no longer duplicated here.
