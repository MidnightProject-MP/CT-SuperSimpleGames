I have watched toddlers interact with their surroundings, especially smartphones, and their instinctive tap-happy behavior is fascinating but also a little scary. Smartphones are not really designed for that kind of interaction.

I want to create SuperSimpleGames, a series of mobile-first games toddlers can interact with safely and intuitively. They should feel like games, but underneath they should support developmental skills in disguise.

Think colors, numbers, letters, size, matching, cause and effect, and similar early concepts.

The experience should be big, high-contrast, shiny, simple, forgiving, and resilient, almost the opposite of most modern apps and games. A toddler should be able to tap freely and still have a satisfying experience rather than constantly doing something wrong.

I have not yet defined the individual games, exact age range, progression model, or overall structure.
